5. Investment basics

  • Need it within a year? Consider accessible savings at an FDIC-insured bank rather than stocks, because markets can drop right before you need the money.
  • Need it in 1–5 years? Be cautious with stock-market risk. Keep essential goal money in lower-risk options appropriate to your timeframe.
  • 5+ year goal? If your emergency savings and expensive debt are handled, learn about diversified, low-cost index funds through a reputable regulated brokerage.
  • Retirement money? Retirement accounts can have tax benefits but rules and withdrawal restrictions apply; don't choose one for money you'll need soon.
  • Avoid “quick money” promises: don't use essential cash for meme coins, leverage, or anything promising guaranteed returns.
